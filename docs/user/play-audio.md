# Play audio on your computer or a Yoto player

Listen to tracks in OpenMyo on your computer, or send them to a Yoto player. Playback keeps going while you move between screens.

## Prerequisites

- To play on a Yoto player: you're signed in to Yoto, and the player is online.

## Play a track on your computer

1. Hover a track in the playlist editor or on the **Import** screen and select ▶. The rest of the playlist plays after it. Import previews play your local files before anything is uploaded.
2. Use the bar along the bottom of the window: play/pause, previous, next, a position slider and volume.

The keyboard's media keys and Cmd+Shift+Space (Ctrl+Shift+Space on Windows) also play and pause.

## Play on a Yoto player

1. While something plays, select the speaker button in the bottom-right corner of the bar. It lists **This computer** and your Yoto players. Offline players are greyed out.
2. Choose an idle player. What's playing here moves to it, at the same track and position. A player that's already playing carries on undisturbed.

Until you switch back, every ▶ plays on that player. The bar shows its track, play/pause, next/previous, position and volume.

## Control a player that's already playing

Yoto players that are playing also show as a line each in the bar, with pause, volume and **Control**. **Control** makes that player the output without changing what it plays.

> [!NOTE]
> OpenMyo always starts on **This computer**, so opening it never plays out loud in another room.

## Sign in again for player control

The first time after player control arrives, Yoto asks you to sign in once more to allow OpenMyo to control your players.
